🕌 The Pillar of Salah

Salah — the ritual prayer performed five times daily — is the second pillar of Islam and the most consistent act of worship prescribed upon every Muslim. It is a direct, unmediated conversation between the servant and his Lord, renewed five times every single day without exception.

The Prophet ﷺ described Salah as the pillar of the religion. When a person abandons it, they have struck at the very foundation of their faith. When a person guards it — protecting its times, its conditions, its presence of heart — they stand in the ranks of those closest to Allah.

Salah was not revealed through Jibreel (عليه السلام) like the rest of the Quran and commands of the religion. It was given directly to the Prophet ﷺ on the night of Al-Isra wal-Mi'raj. This distinction alone tells the believer everything about its weight.

🌙 The Night Prayer — Tahajjud

Tahajjud is the voluntary night prayer performed after sleeping and waking before Fajr. It is the highest of the voluntary prayers in rank, and the most beloved of the nawāfil to Allah. The Prophet ﷺ never abandoned it throughout his life, whether in health or in travel.

The time of Tahajjud is the last third of the night — when the world is silent, the nafs is soft, and Allah descends to the lowest heaven calling out: "Is there anyone supplicating so that I may respond? Is there anyone seeking forgiveness so that I may forgive?" The believer who wakes at this hour has answered a call that most others sleep through.

Tahajjud is not merely extra ibadah. It is the distinguishing mark of the serious servant — the one who loves Allah beyond obligation, who gives up comfortable sleep for the sweetness of standing before his Lord in darkness.

✦
The Quran

كَانُوا قَلِيلًا مِّنَ اللَّيْلِ مَا يَهْجَعُونَ ۝ وَبِالْأَسْحَارِ هُمْ يَسْتَغْفِرُونَ

🌙 Sawm — The Fast

Sawm — fasting — is the fourth pillar of Islam, obligatory upon every Muslim in the month of Ramadan and recommended throughout the year on designated days. It is not merely abstaining from food and drink. The Prophet ﷺ warned that whoever does not abandon falsehood and acting upon it, Allah has no need of him giving up his food and drink.

Fasting purifies the nafs, breaks the dominance of the lower self, and creates taqwa — God-consciousness — which is precisely what Allah mentions as its purpose in the Quran. The fasting person tames the desires that separate him from his Lord.

Voluntary fasting — on Mondays and Thursdays, the 13th, 14th, and 15th of each lunar month, and the day of Arafah — are among the most beloved of deeds to Allah. The Prophet ﷺ fasted so frequently that his companions wondered when he ever broke fast.

🌿 Sunnah Salah — The Confirmed Prayers

The Sunnah prayers are the voluntary prayers the Prophet ﷺ consistently performed alongside the obligatory prayers. They are of two types: the Rawātib — the confirmed sunnahs attached to the five daily prayers — and the non-attached sunnahs performed at other times.

The Prophet ﷺ said that there is no Muslim servant who prays twelve rakāt of voluntary prayer every day, over and above the obligatory prayers, except that Allah builds for him a house in Paradise. These twelve are: 2 before Fajr, 4 before Dhuhr and 2 after, 2 after Maghrib, and 2 after Isha.

The Sunnah prayers serve as a cover for deficiencies in the obligatory prayer. On the Day of Judgment, when the obligatory prayer is found incomplete, Allah will say: "Look and see if My servant has any voluntary prayers that can complete what is missing from his obligatory prayers."

✨ Nafl Salah — The Supererogatory Prayer

Nafl Salah refers to any voluntary prayer beyond the obligatory and the confirmed Sunnah prayers. It is the overflow of a heart that cannot get enough of standing before Allah — praying not out of obligation or routine, but out of love and longing.

Among the most virtuous Nafl prayers are: Salat Ad-Duha (the forenoon prayer, 2 to 12 rakāt), Salat Al-Awwabeen (6 rakāt after Maghrib), Tahiyyatul Masjid (2 rakāt upon entering the mosque), and Salat Al-Istikhārah (2 rakāt when seeking guidance in a matter).

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever draws near to Me through supererogatory deeds, I will love him." The Nafl prayer is the primary vehicle through which a servant earns the nearness and love of Allah beyond what duty requires.

💛 Sadaqah — Voluntary Charity

Sadaqah is voluntary charity given for the sake of Allah alone — beyond the obligatory Zakat. It is one of the greatest deeds a Muslim can perform, extinguishing sin as water extinguishes fire, warding off calamity, and multiplying in reward with Allah in ways the mind cannot measure.

The Prophet ﷺ was the most generous of people, and his generosity increased dramatically in Ramadan. He gave without fear of poverty, because he understood the promise of Allah: that every dirham given in charity, Allah replaces manifold. Sadaqah is not a loss — it is the most profitable investment a believer can make.

Sadaqah takes many forms: wealth, a smile, removing harm from a path, giving good counsel, and speaking a kind word. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Every act of goodness is sadaqah." But wealth-based sadaqah carries a special weight, as it is hardest for the nafs and most visible in its sincerity.

🏃 Exercise — The Body's Right

The body is a trust. It will be asked about on a day when every trust is accounted for. Neglecting it is not humility — it is negligence. Physical strength is not vanity; it is the capacity to worship, to serve, to carry responsibility without breaking. A person who cannot sustain their own health becomes a burden to others, and a burden to their own ambitions.

Exercise is not about aesthetics. It is about function. A body that moves regularly has a stronger heart, clearer mind, more stable mood, and longer productive life. Sedentary living now carries mortality risk comparable to smoking — and it does its damage quietly, over years, without a single dramatic symptom until it is too late to easily reverse.

Consistency governs everything. Thirty minutes of brisk movement five days a week produces measurable cardiovascular benefit. Two sessions of resistance training weekly preserve muscle and bone density that decline with age. You do not need a gym membership or an optimized programme to start — you need to start.

✦
Pillars of Health
What Actually Moves the Needle
🏃 Movement
150+ minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week — walking, cycling, swimming, stair climbing. Add two sessions of bodyweight or resistance training to preserve muscle as you age. The threshold is low. Most people are not clearing it.
🥗 Nutrition
Whole foods over processed ones. Lean protein at every meal. Vegetables and legumes as the bulk. Limit sugar, refined carbohydrates, and ultra-processed oils. Eat until 80% full — the gut signals satiety with a 20-minute delay, so the last 20% is almost always overshoot.
😴 Sleep
7–9 hours per night. Sleep is when muscle repairs, memory consolidates, and hormones regulate. Chronic sleep deprivation — even at 6 hours — accelerates biological aging, impairs judgment, and increases appetite for junk food. It is not a virtue to sleep less.
💧 Hydration
2–3 litres of water daily. The body signals dehydration late — by the time you feel thirst, focus and physical output are already impaired. Start the morning with a full glass of water before anything else. Coffee and tea do not replace water.
